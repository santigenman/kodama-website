01 — About

Wood has
a spirit.
We listen
to it.

Kodama is a design and woodworking studio based in Todos Santos, Baja California Sur.

We design and build custom furniture, kitchens, cabinetry, doors, and architectural millwork for residential and commercial spaces. Every project is made to order, combining thoughtful design, traditional craftsmanship, and modern fabrication techniques.

Rather than producing standard pieces, we work closely with architects, interior designers, and homeowners to develop solutions that respond to each space and the way it will be lived in. From the first sketch to the final installation, every detail is considered with longevity, function, and material integrity in mind.

Wood is at the center of our work. We believe it deserves to be understood, respected, and used honestly. Every board has its own character, and every project is an opportunity to create something that feels timeless rather than temporary.

The name Kodama comes from Japanese folklore, where a kodama is the spirit that inhabits a tree. It reflects our respect for the material and our belief that well-crafted objects carry a lasting presence long after they leave the workshop.

Based in Todos Santos since 2018, we work on projects throughout Baja California Sur and beyond.
