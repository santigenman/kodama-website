02 — How it works

From first visit
to final install.

One team handles your project from start to finish, so what we draw is what we build.

01

Visit & measure

We come to your home or job site, take measurements and talk through how you'll use the space.

02

Design & quote

You get drawings, wood and finish options, and a clear quote with a timeline.

03

Build

Everything is made in our Todos Santos workshop, in solid wood with traditional joinery.

04

Install

We deliver and install ourselves, and adjust on site until every door and drawer runs right.
